01 // Ground Truth

A real person figuring things out, learning by prototyping, and testing assumptions against the physical world.

I am not interested in curated corporate bios or inflated teenage startup theater. I am a 15-year-old student currently in Class 11. My days are split between school coursework, building experiential learning systems at Project Polaris, and spending late nights reading aircraft manuals, wing aerodynamics, and physics proofs.

Aviation captured me because the sky does not negotiate with ego. An airplane flies only when lift overcomes weight and thrust overcomes drag. That unforgiving precision is the same rigor I try to bring to education and everything else I touch: strip away empty jargon, build the actual prototype, and see if it holds altitude.
